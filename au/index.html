How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a skill or a whole year

    Search the skill map, or choose a whole-year check-up from Kindergarten to Year 6.

  2. 2

    Your child answers a few questions

    A wrong answer gets one more, easier try. Two misses and it checks the skills that come before. Young children can have each question read aloud.

  3. 3

    See where the gap starts

    Results show what your child has got, what is shaky, and what to practise next, in order from the earliest year.
